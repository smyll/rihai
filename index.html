How to use the result

“Application due” is a prompt to verify records and approach the court. Review prior convictions, amended charges, other proceedings and accused-caused delay. Multiple charges remain in legal review; qualifying rows can prepare a separate request asking the court to determine section 479(2). Unresolved penalties still require record review. An outer-limit alert concerns the listed case and does not cancel another case's remand order.

The supplied penalty table is a prototype reference. Read the BNSS text. Review the calculation policy.
